# Legends Log Rules

## What Is The Legends Log?

The Legends Log is a record of every individual and organization backing Type 2 Labs' mission to build the first streaming base on the moon.

By backing, you pay to be listed on the log.

---

## How to Back

1. Back any amount ($1 minimum) at [type2labs.co](https://type2labs.co)
2. Submit your legend details (name, callsign, and tier-appropriate perks)
3. Your position is assigned based on when you first back—first come, first served

Your position number is fixed and will not change for as long as the log exists. Legend #5 will always be #5.

---

## Tier System

Your tier is determined by your cumulative lifetime backing.

| Tier | Backing | Perks |
|------|---------|-------|
| 00 | $1+ | Name on Legends Log, exclusive Discord |
| 01 | $10+ | + Callsign |
| 02 | $100+ | + Message |

Additional perks may be added in the future and may apply to existing backers.

---

## Callsign Rules

Your callsign is your unique identifier in the log.

- **Unique**: Only one backer can hold a callsign at a time
- **Changeable**: You may request a callsign change (subject to availability)
- **Format**: 3-20 characters, letters, numbers, and underscores only
- **Family-friendly**: No profanity, slurs, or inappropriate content
- **Non-transferable**: Callsigns cannot be sold or transferred to others

---

## Position Rules

Your position number rewards early belief.

- Position is assigned when you first back—in order
- Position is **fixed** and will not change for as long as the log exists
- You can upgrade your tier with additional backing
- Your position stays the same regardless of tier

Legend #1 believed first. Legend #1000 believed before Legend #1001. This order is preserved for as long as the log exists.

---

## Tier Upgrades

Your tier is calculated based on total backing received.

- Each payment is a separate transaction for log listing at your current tier
- When you reach a new tier threshold, your listing is updated
- There is no obligation to continue backing
- There is no guarantee of reaching a future tier
- Your position never changes—only your tier and perks

---

## Stage Tags

Your tag shows when you believed.

- **α (Alpha)**: Prototype stage—current stage
- Future stages will be added as the mission progresses

Backing during earlier stages shows you believed before it was proven. Alpha backers will always carry the α tag.

## What Is A Streaming Base?

A streaming base is not just rovers on the moon. It is permanent infrastructure:

- **Fixed location**: A designated spot on the lunar surface
- **Solar power**: Panels to generate electricity
- **Charging station**: Infrastructure to power rovers and equipment
- **Regolith structure**: A physical structure built from lunar material

The base streams 24/7 (during lunar day) and serves as a permanent outpost for remote exploration.

This definition is subject to change as the mission evolves.
